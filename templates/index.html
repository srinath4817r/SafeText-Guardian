    DEEP DIVE EXPLAINER

    How SafeText Guardian Works

    A 4-stage pipeline combining local privacy masking, deterministic heuristics, and Google Gemma 4 AI reasoning.

    01

    Smart Data Redaction

    Personal numbers and secret codes are masked locally on the server before AI evaluation. Indian 10-digit mobile numbers and OTP digits are filtered while legitimate links and amounts remain intact for analysis.

    02

    Heuristic Rules Engine

    Inspects hostnames for brand impersonation against genuine Indian institutions (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Paytm, Income Tax), identifies URL shorteners (bit.ly, tinyurl), risky TLDs, and detects psychological urgency phrases.

    03

    Google Gemma 4 Reasoning

    Powered by Google's open-weight Gemma 4 model via the Gemini API. Evaluates untrusted sender context, detects parcel delivery traps, electricity cutoff scams, and subtle social engineering that bypasses rigid filters.

    04

    Plain-Language Verdict

    Generates an unequivocal Low, Medium, or High risk verdict with zero technical jargon. Provides plain language explanations in English, Hindi, or Telugu, actionable next steps, and a one-tap family alert.

    ENGINEERED FOR FAMILIES

    Built to Shield Everyday Users

    Designed with accessibility and high-contrast liquid ergonomics so older adults never fall prey to phishing tricks.

    Multi-Modal Screenshot Scanner

    Don't want to type or tap on risky links? Just upload or take a screenshot of your WhatsApp chat, SMS inbox, or bank alert. Gemma 4 reads and parses the screenshot safely.

    • Supports PNG, JPG, and WebP up to 5 MB
    • Reads blurred, cropped, and vernacular screenshots
    • Extracts embedded web addresses and short links

    Native Multilingual Voice Readout

    Complex bank alerts shouldn't cause panic. SafeText Guardian translates explanations into English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Telugu (తెలుగు) with native browser text-to-speech audio.

    • High accessibility for elderly grandparents
    • Adjustable pacing for clarity and comfort
    • Complete vernacular UI text synchronization

    Indian Scam Pattern Radar

    Trained against synthetic real-world Indian cyber crime formats: fake KYC deadline countdowns, APK malware downloads, electricity cut-off notices, and counterfeit reward portals.

    • Genuine debit/credit alerts recognized without false alarms
    • Phishing lookalike checks against official government portals
    • Instant recommendations to dial 1930 for financial recovery
    ZERO RETENTION PROMISE

    Your Privacy & Security

    Complete transparency in how your queries are handled and evaluated.

    Zero Server Logging

    We do not store, log, or record user messages or uploaded screenshots on our servers or databases. Once analysis finishes, memory is immediately released.

    API Transmission Notice

    Messages and images are transmitted to Google's Gemini API endpoints for analysis by Gemma 4. Screenshots are NOT masked before upload, so avoid uploading images displaying active OTPs or personal Aadhaar/PAN cards.

    No Trackers or Analytics

    SafeText Guardian includes zero advertising trackers, third-party user fingerprinting, or marketing cookies. You get clean, uninterrupted protection.
