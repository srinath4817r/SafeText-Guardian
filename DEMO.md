# Demo Script: SafeText Guardian (2 Minutes)

**0:00 - 0:30: Introduction & Simple Use Case**
- Open `http://localhost:5000`.
- Paste a legitimate message: "Your account XX1234 has been credited with Rs 2000."
- Click "Analyze Message".
- Show the **Green Low Risk Badge** and the explanation.

**0:30 - 1:15: Scam Detection (The "Meat")**
- Click "Try Example".
- Paste a scam: "Your SBI account is blocked. Update KYC now at http://sbi-kyc-update.xyz/claim"
- Click "Analyze Message".
- Point out the **Red High Risk Badge**.
- Show the **Link Check** section: highlight the "Risky TLD" and "Brand Lookalike" detections.
- Use the **Read Aloud** button to demonstrate accessibility for seniors.

**1:15 - 1:45: Image Upload & Multi-language**
- Upload a screenshot of a scam message.
- Select "Hindi" from the dropdown.
- Analyze and show the explanation written in Hindi.

**1:45 - 2:00: Next Steps & Family Warning**
- Show the "Next Steps" card with a clear warning.
- Click "Warn my family" to show how it generates a pre-filled WhatsApp message.
